Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is this AI-powered?

No. It uses local text extraction, simple rules, and keyword similarity. No AI model is connected.

Does my resume leave my computer?

The browser sends the file to a Python server on your computer. It is analyzed in memory, not saved or sent to a third party.

Why might a PDF not work?

Scanned PDFs contain images instead of selectable text. OCR is not included.

Is this a hiring decision?

No. It cannot guarantee ATS performance or predict an interview.
